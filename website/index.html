About Kwaku Online Store

Kwaku Online Store is a growing e-commerce business. This website is a demonstration: the shopping cart lives in your browser and no real orders or payments are processed. It is packaged as a Docker container, stored in Amazon ECR and served by several Kubernetes pods on Amazon EKS behind an AWS load balancer.
